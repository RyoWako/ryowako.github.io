Personal Concierge

Personal Concierge is a private tool used by its owner to organize travel reservations and answer questions about upcoming trips. It is not offered to other users.

With the owner's permission, it uses read-only Gmail access to find reservation confirmation and change emails. It extracts relevant booking details into notes stored on the owner's computer. The tool cannot send or change email.

When the owner asks a travel question, relevant booking details may be provided to the AI service the owner uses to generate an answer.
