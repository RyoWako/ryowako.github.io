Privacy Policy - Personal Concierge

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Who uses this tool

Personal Concierge is used only by its owner with the owner's own Google account.

Google data accessed

The tool requests the gmail.readonly scope. This permits it to read Gmail messages and metadata, but not to send, edit, mark as read, archive, or delete messages. The tool searches for travel reservation confirmations and updates, including flights, lodging, trains, and rental cars.

Use and storage

Relevant booking details, such as dates, destinations, providers, and confirmation numbers, are extracted into notes on the owner's computer. Full email bodies are not stored in those notes. OAuth credentials are stored separately on the owner's computer. The owner may delete the local booking notes and rebuild them from Gmail.

AI processing and sharing

When the owner asks a question about travel plans, relevant extracted booking details may be sent to the AI service configured by the owner to generate an answer. Gmail data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with other users. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and access control

Booking notes remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Gmail access can be revoked at any time in the owner's Google Account connections. Deleting the local OAuth token also stops this tool from accessing Gmail until the owner authorizes it again.
