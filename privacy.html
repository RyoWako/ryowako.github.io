Privacy Policy - Personal Concierge Travel

Last updated: 2026-09-28

Who uses this app

This app is used only by its developer, for their own Google account.

Data accessed

Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly). Only reservation confirmation emails (hotels, flights, trains, rental cars) are processed.

How data is used and stored

Booking details (dates, place, confirmation number) are extracted and saved as notes on the owner's own computer. Email bodies are not stored. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's API.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, and it is not used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
