Personal Concierge Health

Personal Concierge Health is a personal, single-user tool built and used by Ryo Wako. It reads the owner's own Google Health data (sleep, weight, body fat, activity, and nutrition log) using read-only scopes, to produce personal trend summaries and meal suggestions for the owner.

It is not offered to anyone else.
