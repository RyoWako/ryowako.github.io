Privacy Policy - Personal Concierge Health

Effective date: 2026-10-22

Who uses this tool

Personal Concierge Health is a personal, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner, Ryo Wako, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google data accessed

The tool requests only these read-only scopes:

  • googlehealth.sleep.readonly
  • health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly
  • nutrition.readonly
  • activity_and_fitness.readonly

It cannot create, change, or delete any health data.

Use and storage

The data is used only to produce personal trend summaries and meal suggestions for the owner. It is stored only on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

The data is not shared with or sold to third parties, and is not used for advertising. It may be processed by the owner's AI assistant (Anthropic Claude) only to produce the owner's own summaries.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
